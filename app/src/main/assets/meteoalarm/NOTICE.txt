The files in this folder were changed: they were built by tools/meteoalarm_areas.py from
geocodes.json of the "meteoalarm" Python package. The outlines were simplified with a
tolerance of 0.001 degrees, the coordinates rounded to 4 decimal places, and the names of
the areas left out. The unchanged file is available at
https://github.com/NiklasJordan/meteoalarm/blob/main/src/meteoalarm/assets/geocodes.json
